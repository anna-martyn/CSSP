Fig. SX. Bacterial load in rhizosphere and root samples of Lotus japonicus wild-type and CSSP mutant plants inoculated with the Lotus SynCom. Synthetic spike-in DNA (10⁴ copies) was added to the first PCR of each sample. Only ASVs assigned to SynCom members were considered as bacterial reads. (A) Number of spike-in reads per sample. (B) Total bacterial load, calculated as the ratio of bacterial to spike-in reads multiplied by the number of spike-in copies. (C) Commensal bacterial load, calculated as total load multiplied by the relative abundance of commensal ASVs. Symbionts, defined as Rhizobiales ASVs with ≥1% mean relative abundance in wild-type nodules (Mesorhizobium spp.), were excluded. (D) As in C, but with all ASVs with ≥1% mean relative abundance in wild-type nodules (including the non-rhizobial co-colonisers Variovorax and Acidovorax) excluded. Dots represent biological replicates (n = 4). Mutants were compared with the wild-type within each compartment using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests with Benjamini–Hochberg correction (adjusted P ≥ 0.05, ns = non-significant). Y-axes are log₁₀-scaled.
