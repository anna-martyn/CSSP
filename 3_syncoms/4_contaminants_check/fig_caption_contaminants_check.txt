Fig. SX. Non-SynCom reads in the SynCom experiments and the effect of a likely seed-borne Enterobacter strain on Hordeum wild-type communities. (A) Share of reads (%) assigned to non-SynCom ASVs per sample in the Lotus japonicus and Hordeum vulgare SynCom experiments, shown by genotype and compartment. Non-SynCom ASVs were removed before all downstream analyses. Each mutant was compared with wild-type using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction (ns = non-significant). One Hordeum ccamk rhizosphere sample with fewer than 1000 reads was excluded. (B) PCoA of Hordeum SynCom communities based on Bray-Curtis-dissimilarities, calculated separately for each compartment using SynCom ASVs only. Triangles mark wild-type replicates carrying the Enterobacter strain (>5% of reads before removal; taxonomy assigned using BLAST). (C) Number of differentially abundant SynCom isolates per mutant and compartment (MaAsLin2 and structural zeros, Fig. 6) that show the same direction of change (min. 1.4-fold) when each mutant is compared only with the two wild-type replicates without Enterobacter. This is a descriptive comparison (n=2), not a statistical test.
